## Strategic Story

We have two products to sell, and they reinforce each other:

1. **GoCardless** — the payment-collection service itself. Direct Debit / bank-to-bank pull payments. Sells on cashflow, time saving, reduced bad-debt risk.

2. **The GoCardless integration app** (Opera-side automation we've built) — turns GoCardless from "another platform to log into" into a one-click step inside Opera. Sells on zero re-keying, automatic reconciliation, no manual matching.

The two products land on the same buyer at the same time: an Opera user who currently chases payments by phone / email / cheque-and-wait. GoCardless gives them the collection method. The integration app makes it painless inside their existing system.

**The bigger story** — and the one that re-positions Opera in the market:

> Opera is no longer the legacy ERP it's accused of being. We're building a **finance-automation suite that lives on top of Opera** — GoCardless integration, AI-driven bank reconciliation, supplier-statement automation, and more to come. The combination of *Opera as the system of record* + *our automation layer* is a compelling alternative to ripping-and-replacing with a cloud-native ERP. It costs less, preserves a decade of process knowledge, and delivers the same automation outcomes.

This is the framing we want every prospect to walk away with. GoCardless is the wedge — it's concrete, measurable (£X per month saved), and obviously useful. Once they're in, we can sell the rest of the suite.

## Audience Segments

### Primary — Existing Opera installed base

The single largest, warmest, most addressable audience. They already paid for Opera; they're already invested. They have the exact pain points we solve. Pegasus and the Pegasus reseller channel reach them.

| Segment | Sub-pain | Hook |
|---|---|---|
| **SME finance directors** (turnover £1M–£20M) | Cashflow tight; AR clerk spends 1–2 days/week chasing | "Get paid faster — automated DD from inside Opera" |
| **Bookkeepers / accountancy practices using Opera for clients** | Per-client manual reconciliation eats margin | "Reconcile every client's bank statement in 5 minutes — AI does it" |
| **Owner-operators of small businesses** | Time poor; no dedicated AR/AP | "Stop chasing money. Direct Debit collects it for you, Opera posts it automatically" |

### Secondary — Pegasus reseller channel

Resellers sell Opera. If they can also sell our GoCardless integration as an add-on (or co-sell with us), it broadens their offer and gives them a story for "what's new". They want stickier customers — automation = stickier.

### Tertiary — Net-new SME finance buyers (general pitch)

For people not yet on Opera. The pitch is *"if you're already considering finance software, here's a complete automated finance suite — Opera + our automation layer"*. Reframes Opera (which they may have heard of as legacy) as the foundation for a modern automated stack. Not the priority audience — but the assets we build for primary work for this audience too.

## Value Propositions (layered)

The pitch lands in three concentric layers. Lead with the surface; prospects who engage hear the deeper layers.

### Layer 1 — Surface (cashflow, immediate measurable)

- "Collect customer payments by Direct Debit."
- "20–30% faster cash collection vs. invoice-and-chase."
- "Bad debt drops to near zero — fees are pennies vs. a chargeback."
- "Customer-friendly — no card-on-file expiry; UK Direct Debit Guarantee."

### Layer 2 — Integration (zero manual work)

- "Push 'Collect' from inside Opera. Customer payments are pulled, posted, and reconciled in one workflow."
- "No CSV exports. No re-keying. No standalone GoCardless dashboard to manage."
- "Mandate management, payment requests, statement matching — all in Opera."
- "Works with how you already work — Opera is still your system of record."

### Layer 3 — Automation suite (re-frame Opera)

- "GoCardless is one of a growing set of automation tools we've built on Opera."
- "Bank reconciliation: AI extracts statements, matches to Opera, reconciles in minutes."
- "Supplier statements: AI matches your supplier statements to your purchase ledger."
- "Customer-statement-driven cash collection."
- "Automated reporting and dashboards."
- "Together: Opera + automation = a modern, fully-featured finance platform without changing core systems."

## ROI Story (for the customer)

The pitch deck claims £21k/yr savings. We need to back this with a calculator the prospect runs themselves. Inputs:

| Input | Default | Source |
|---|---|---|
| # invoices/month | 100 | Customer enters |
| Avg time spent chasing per invoice | 8 min | Industry benchmark |
| Hourly cost of AR clerk | £25 | Industry benchmark |
| Avg invoice value | £500 | Customer enters |
| Avg days late (current) | 14 days | Customer enters |
| Cost of working capital | 8% APR | Default |
| GoCardless transaction fee | 1% capped £4 | Public pricing |

Outputs:

- **Time saved**: # invoices × 8min × hourly rate × 12 → £/yr
- **Working capital saved**: # invoices × invoice value × 14 days × 8% / 365 × 12 → £/yr
- **Bad debt reduction**: assume 1.5% of revenue → 0.5% (DD halves it) → £/yr
- **Less GoCardless fees**: # invoices × invoice value × 1% capped → -£/yr
- **Net annual saving**: shown prominently

Build this as a single-page interactive calculator. Drop on landing page. Email auto-sends a personalised PDF report when the prospect submits.

## Risks and Mitigations

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Pegasus / partner channel resists co-marketing | High — kills installed-base reach | Open with reseller-friendly terms; offer co-branded materials |
| GoCardless platform changes API/policy | High — breaks our integration | We control the integration; we adapt. Have direct GC partner contact. |
| Customer perceives Opera as "still legacy" despite suite | Medium — slows net-new acquisition | Lead with concrete cashflow win, not abstract "modernisation" pitch |
| Existing-base saturation (limited installed base) | Medium — caps Phase 1 ceiling | Move to Phase 2 (resellers) and Phase 4 (net-new) earlier if Phase 1 saturates |
| Internal capacity (we are 2 people) | High — restricts execution speed | Outsource specific deliverables (case study writing, ad creative). Prioritise P1 assets only. |
| Legal / compliance for marketing claims (e.g. "20% faster cashflow") | Medium — could trigger disputes | Cite source / averages; avoid superlative absolute claims. Show as ranges. |

## Pricing Considerations (separate decision but informs marketing)

The plan above assumes pricing is decided — it isn't. Anchor points:

- **Per-tenant subscription**: £50–200/month for GoCardless integration. Tiered by transaction volume.
- **Setup / onboarding fee**: £500 one-off (could be waived for Phase 1 launch period).
- **Reseller margin**: 20–30% recurring.
- **Suite bundle (Phase 3)**: 30% discount vs. à-la-carte for taking 3+ apps.

These need confirming before any pricing appears on marketing assets. Until then assets should say "From £X/month — contact us for pricing".

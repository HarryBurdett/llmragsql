The Problem Every Finance Team Knows

Supplier account management is one of the most time-consuming tasks in any accounts department. It's repetitive, manual, and takes your team away from higher-value work.

Today's Reality

  • Supplier statements arrive by email and sit unread
  • Manual line-by-line comparison against your ledger
  • Spreadsheets or printouts to track differences
  • Drafting emails to query missing invoices
  • Chasing suppliers who don't respond to queries
  • No record of what was communicated and when
  • Bank detail fraud attempts go undetected
  • Accidental overpayments from unreconciled accounts

With Automation

  • Statements detected and processed automatically
  • AI reads every line and matches to your ledger
  • Differences identified and categorised in seconds
  • Professional response emails drafted automatically
  • Overdue queries escalated with follow-up reminders
  • Complete audit trail of every communication
  • Bank detail changes flagged instantly
  • Accounts reconciled before you open the post

The average accounts team spends 12-16 hours per month on supplier statement reconciliation alone. That's 2 full working days every month on repetitive manual work.

Smart Business Rules

Not all differences are equal. The system applies intelligent rules to protect your cash position — only querying items that are not in your favour.

⚠ Query (Not In Your Favour)

  • Invoice on their statement, not in your system
  • Their amount is higher than yours
  • Credit note they claim was sent but you haven't received
  • Payment they say was received but you can't match

These items are raised as queries in the response email. The supplier is asked to provide documentation.

😶 Stay Quiet (In Your Favour)

  • Invoice on your records they've forgotten
  • Their amount is lower than yours
  • Credit note you haven't claimed
  • Overpayment in your favour

These items are not mentioned in the response. No point reminding a supplier about money they owe you or credits they've forgotten.

💬 Always Notify

  • Payments made (always helpful to confirm)
  • Upcoming payment schedule
  • Large discrepancies (flagged for review)
  • Unusual patterns (held for manual check)

Payment notifications reduce inbound calls. Suppliers want to know when they're getting paid.

The result: your cash position is protected

The system never reminds a supplier about money they owe you, credits they've forgotten, or invoices they haven't billed. It only queries items where you might be overpaying or missing a credit you're entitled to.

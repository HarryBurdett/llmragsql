Built-In Safeguards

+

🛡️ Mandate Verification

Every payment is verified against the GoCardless mandate before posting. If a payment doesn't match the linked Opera customer, it's blocked automatically.

🔒 Duplicate Prevention

Four layers of protection: local tracking, GoCardless API check, Opera unallocated credit detection, and mandate-level validation. No customer is ever charged twice.

📋 Complete Audit Trail

Every payment request, collection, and Opera posting is logged with full detail. From invoice to bank receipt — complete traceability for auditors.

🏦 Direct Debit Guarantee

Your customers are protected by the UK Direct Debit Guarantee. They can claim a full refund at any time — giving them confidence to set up mandates.

Configurable BACS Reference

Your company name and invoice reference appear on your customer's bank statement — so they always know what they're paying for.
